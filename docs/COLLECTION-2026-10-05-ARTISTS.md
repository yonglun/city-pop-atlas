# Five artists, six albums, eight songs

This bounded expansion adds Akina Nakamori, Junichi Inagaki, Meiko Nakahara, Takako Mamiya and Makoto Matsushita. Existing labels and aliases were checked before adding new identities.

## Musical scope

- Akina Nakamori: BITTER AND SWEET (1985), CRIMSON (1986), Romantic na Yoru Dawa, UNSTEADY LOVE, OH NO, OH YES!, Eki. EPO, Toshiki Kadomatsu, Nobuyuki Shimizu and Mariya Takeuchi connections use song-specific credits. Selected works show a City Pop intersection within a wider pop/kayōkyoku career.
- Junichi Inagaki: SHYLIGHTS (1983), Dramatic Rain (1982 single). Akira Inoue is the album’s main arranger; this is not an all-tracks credit. Dramatic Rain credits Yasushi Akimoto, Kyohei Tsutsumi and Motoki Funayama.
- Meiko Nakahara: 2時までのシンデレラ -FRIDAY MAGIC- (1982), FANTASY. Nakahara’s writing/singing and Hiroshi Shinkawa’s arrangement remain distinct.
- Takako Mamiya: LOVE TRIP (1982), its opening title song. Etsuko Kisugi and Kazuo Shiina remain verified credit text rather than being silently omitted or fabricated as new incomplete people nodes.
- Makoto Matsushita: FIRST LIGHT (1981), its opening title song. The original release retains year precision; later provider dates do not replace it.
