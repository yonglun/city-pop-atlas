# Five further singers and their musical connections

This collection adds five identities absent from the current catalog and its aliases: Yurie Kokubu, CINDY, Mari Iijima, Rajie and Kaoru Sudo. Each has one representative album and one song, with a separate original essay in Chinese, English and Japanese and its own conceptual illustration. Kaoru Sudo is a different person from the already cataloged Kaoru Akimoto.

## Artists and representative works

- **Yurie Kokubu:** *Relief 72 hours* (1983) and “とばしてTaxi Man.” Tetsuji Hayashi’s album production is separate from the song’s writing and arrangement credits; the composer is Ken Sato, not the actor suggested by a provider’s mistaken romanization.
- **CINDY:** *Angel Touch* (1990) and “Surprise.” Her composing and singing roles are represented separately. Chinfa Kan’s lyric credit belongs to the song. Tatsuro Yamashita’s backing-vocal participation is an album-level credit, with no inferred track assignment. The 1990 date is retained rather than forcing a City Pop album into the 1980s.
- **Mari Iijima:** *Rosé* (1983) and “Blueberry Jam.” Her songwriting and performance remain distinct from Ryuichi Sakamoto’s production and arrangement. Her earlier voice-acting work is not erased by the date of her singer-songwriter album debut.
- **Rajie:** *Heart to Heart* (1977) and “HOLD ME TIGHT.” Yukihiro Takahashi and Tsugutoshi Goto’s production credits are retained; Goto’s documented song arrangement connects to the existing graph. The original release has year precision, without adopting a streaming service’s January 1 placeholder.
- **Kaoru Sudo:** *CHEF’S SPECIAL* (1980) and “あなただけ I LOVE YOU.” Eiichi Ohtaki’s words and music and the joint Ohtaki/Masataka Matsutoya arrangement are distinguished. The singer’s 1979 debut, the album’s 1980 release and the song’s later 1981 single issue are different events.

## Edition boundaries and source discrepancies

Five exact physical editions contribute 56 positions: MHCL-30150 (10), DQJL-7116 (11), VICL-62217 (11), MHCL-644 (10), and MHCL-1139 (14). CINDY’s vinyl retains A/B sides and the source’s “Candle Lights” spelling. Sudo’s CD explicitly separates four bonuses, including the live “LOVE AGAIN,” from the original ten-song program.

Sony’s accessible *Heart to Heart* page omits positions 3, 6 and 7. HMV’s listing for the same catalog number supplies these positions, whose attributes retain retailer provenance. The other positions retain the official source. This is not described as an entirely official-source tracklist.

Victor’s 2007 *Rosé* issue and its later same-number repress are recorded distinctly in the notes. Sony’s prose that places SRCL-1834 next to Sudo’s original album date does not establish that as the original LP number; its separate Sony listing identifies a 1991 CD. Kokubu’s date and lyricist-name discrepancies are documented against corroborating primary sources, with explicit scope notes.

Every new edition/position has a three-language introduction, a clearly labeled shared album illustration and a link to the full album essay. A title match provides navigation, not recording or master identity. No new physical position is assigned a recordingId or an inherited streaming player.

## Editorial, images and players

The 15 core entries have original five-paragraph essays in all three languages. Interpretations and imagined visual metaphors are distinguished from biographical testimony. No interviews, quotations or lyrics are invented or reproduced. All 15 images were independently generated and visually inspected; none is presented as a photograph, artist likeness or original sleeve.

Mari Iijima’s genuine pianist portrait is supplied through the intact official Spotify artist widget, with no standalone image download or license claim.

Official player resources are checked for title, performer and version metadata. That verification does not establish successful audio playback, regional availability or physical-master equivalence. CINDY’s mixed-identity artist profile is excluded from portrait certification. Album-cover widgets for Kokubu, Rajie and Sudo are not counted as portraits. No Discogs images or unlicensed standalone publicity photographs are imported.
